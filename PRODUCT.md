# Front Office AI product context

Register: product.

## Purpose and users

The product owner is a basketball-minded general manager who wants to test franchise decisions with a sourced analytical assistant. The first version is a local, single-user research workspace. It should help compare team-specific choices and preserve the owner’s scouting judgment.

## Core principles

- Evidence and date accompany basketball facts.
- Projections and owner opinions have separate labels.
- A missing field stays visibly unavailable.
- Recommendations consider franchise context and the other team’s incentives.
- Build one dependable slice before expanding to advanced modules.

## Interface direction

An analyst reviewing a team file on a laptop in a bright front-office meeting room. Use a restrained, readable workbench with clear tables, compact labels, and a small green accent. The interface should feel like a serious research tool and support long sessions without visual noise.
